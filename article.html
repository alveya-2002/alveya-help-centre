Step 1 — Get oriented

Before you begin, make sure you're logged in to your Alvéya account. You can access the dashboard at app.alveya.io. If you don't have an account yet, see Creating your Alvéya account first.

💡 Tip: You can return to this help article at any time from the Help Centre link in the bottom-left corner of the dashboard.

Step 2 — Follow the setup wizard

On your first login you'll be guided through a short setup wizard. This wizard covers:

  • Adding your business name and logo
  • Choosing your primary service category
  • Connecting a payment method
  • Setting your timezone and currency

You can skip any step and complete it later from Settings → Business Profile.

Step 3 — Explore the dashboard

Once setup is complete, you'll land on the main dashboard. Key areas to know:

  1. Left sidebar — navigate between Contacts, Emails, Website, Courses, and Automations.
  2. Top bar — access notifications, account settings, and quick-add actions.
  3. Overview panel — a snapshot of recent activity, upcoming bookings, and revenue at a glance.

Need more help?

If you get stuck, you can submit a support request from any page using the Submit a request link in the header, or reach us via live chat (available on paid plans).
