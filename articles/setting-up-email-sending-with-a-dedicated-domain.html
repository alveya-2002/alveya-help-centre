Setting Up Email Sending with a Dedicated Domain

Updated June 2026
Back to Email Marketing

Setting Up Email Sending in Alvéya

1. Access Email Services Settings

  • Navigate to the bottom of your account settings.
  • Click on Settings.
  • Select Email Services.

2. Understand Shared Domain Usage

  • Currently, you are using a shared domain for sending emails (e.g., noreply.alvear.io).
  • It is recommended to connect your own domain to avoid issues caused by other users.

    • 3. Create a Dedicated Domain

  • Click on Create Dedicated Domain.
  • Consider using a subdomain (e.g., mg.mydomain.com) for sending emails.

    • 4. Configure MX Records

  • If using a subdomain, ensure you configure the MX records.
  • This helps separate email campaigns from your personal emails.

    • 5. Choose Between Subdomain or Root Domain

  • Decide whether to use a subdomain or keep the current setup based on your email campaign performance.

    • 6. Set Up Your Subdomain

  • If you have a professional email (e.g., through Google Workspace), use a subdomain (e.g., connect.elharrison.design).
  • Click Add and Verify.

    • 7. Add DNS Records

  • You can add records manually or automatically through Cloudflare.
  • Follow the instructions to copy and paste the DNS records.

    • 8. Verify Domain

  • After a few minutes, return to verify your domain.
  • Ensure that the SSL security certificate is issued.

    • 9. Set Dedicated Headers

  • Click on the three buttons and select Set Headers.
  • Add your name/business name and the from email address.

    • 10. Configure Domain for Email Features

  • Go to Domain Configuration.
  • Specify the purpose of the domain (e.g., calendar, payments, campaigns).
  • You can use different subdomains for different features or keep it simple.
