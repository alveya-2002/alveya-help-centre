How to Add a Favicon to Your Website, Funnel or Client Portal

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Written Instructions:

Link to canva template for a favicon

Step 1: Understand the Purpose of a Favicon

  • A favicon is a small icon that represents your website in browser tabs.
  • It helps users identify your website easily among multiple tabs.

Step 2: Upload Your Favicon 

  • Navigate to your media storage.
  • Upload your favicon image file.

Step 3: Get the Favicon URL

  • After uploading, click on the three dots next to your favicon file.
  • Select ‘Get Links’ to copy the URL of the favicon.

Step 4: Access Website Settings

  • Go to your website (e.g., Ginkgo Glow).
  • Click on ‘Settings’ in the website dashboard.

Step 5: Paste the Favicon URL 

  • Find the ‘Favicon URL’ field in the settings.
  • Paste the URL you copied from the media storage.

Step 6: Save Your Changes 

  • Scroll down and click ‘Save’ to apply the changes.
