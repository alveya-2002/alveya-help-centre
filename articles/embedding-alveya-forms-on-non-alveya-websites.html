Embedding Alvéya Forms on Non-Alvéya Websites

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

What is Embedding of Alvéya Forms

Embedding Alvéya forms means integrating a form from Alvéya directly onto your website using your own website editor, allowing visitors to fill it out without being redirected elsewhere. This makes the process seamless, ensuring that opt ins, customer enquiries, or appointment bookings happen right within your branded environment. The best part? You won’t need to switch between multiple platforms or make adjustments in different places—just copy the embed code from Alvéya and paste it into your website editor.

Key Benefits of Embedding Alvéya Forms

Embedding Alvéya forms can significantly improve engagement and lead generation on your website. Here are the key benefits:

  • Capture More Leads: Alvéya forms enable you to collect visitor information directly on your site, making it easier to nurture leads.

  • Cross-Platform Integration: Use the forms on various website platforms such as SquareSpace, Wix, Shopify and WordPress.
  • Seamless CRM Syncing: Automatically sync collected data with your Alvéya CRM for streamlined follow-up and automation.
  • Improved Engagement: Use customizable forms to display targeted messages, promotions, or surveys.
  • Performance Tracking: Leverage Alvéya’s built-in analytics to monitor and optimize the performance of your forms.

    • Where to Find the Embed Link in Alvéya?

    To embed Alvéya forms on your website, you first need to locate the embed link in your Alvéya account. This link provides the script that allows the form to appear on your chosen website. Here’s how you can find it:

    Access Form Builder

    From the dashboard, go to the “Websites & Funnels” section in the main navigation menu. Click on the “Forms” menu located at the top right corner. It will take you to the form builder area.

    Choose or Create a Form

    To embed a form, you can either select an existing one from the list or create a new form. If you’re starting fresh, simply click the “+ Add Form” button to build your form. If you already have a form ready, just select it from the available options.

    Access the Embed Link

    Once your form is ready, go to the "Integrate" tab located at the top right corner. Select the type of form from the  options and click on the Copy Embed Code option. If you want it to be on a page, choose "Inline"

    Ready to Use

    Your embed link is now ready to be pasted into your website’s code, following the platform-specific instructions outlined below.

      WordPress Integration

      WordPress supports embedding scripts via plugins or theme settings, making it versatile for adding pop-up forms.

      Access the Editor

      To begin, navigate to the Pages section in your website’s admin dashboard. This is where you can manage your site’s visual settings and design elements. To get into the editor mode, hover over the page where you wish to embed your Alvéya form and click the Edit button

      Open the Block Menu

      Click the ”+” icon in the editor to reveal the list of available blocks for customizing your page.

      Select the Custom HTML Block

      Scroll down and select the Custom HTML option to insert your own code into the page layout.

      Inserting the Embed Code

      Paste the embed code into the appropriate section within the selected file. Ensure it’s placed correctly between the <head> or <footer> tags for proper functionality. Save your changes once done.

      Update and Test

      Save changes and refresh your website to verify the form appears correctly
