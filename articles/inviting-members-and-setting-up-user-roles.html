Inviting members and setting up user roles

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

This article is step one from the Communities playbook. To continue the process of setting up your community after this step, head back to the playbook here.

Written step by step instructions and links to the templates for this video are below.

WRITTEN INSTRUCTIONS:

1. Logging into Your Community

  • Navigate to the Communities tab in Elvaya.
  • Click the big blue login button.
  • Optionally, bookmark the community tab for easy access.

2. Inviting Members to Your Community

  • Click on ‘Invite Members’ in the right-hand corner.
  • Choose to invite members via:

    • Sharing the group link.

    • Inviting them by email.
  • Optionally grant admin privileges to new members.

3. Member Sign-Up Process

  • After sending the invite link, new members will see a sign-up screen.
  • They need to enter their full name, email, and create a password.
  • After clicking ‘Sign Up’, they will receive a secure code via email to complete the process.

4. Member Profile Setup

  • New members can upload a photo and write a bio.
  • They have the option to invite other members to the group.

5. Adjusting Member Invitation Settings

  • Go to the admin area of your community.
  • Click on ‘Settings’ and then ‘Details’.
  • To restrict member invitations, untick ‘Allow Members to Invite Members’.
  • Important for paid groups.

6. Setting Group Visibility

  • You can set your group to Private.
  • Control visibility in the SwitchBar for non-group members.

7. Managing User Roles

  • In the Members Area, click on the three dots next to a member’s name.
  • Options include:

    • Change Role (Admin or Contributor).

    • Ban from the group.
    • Manage course access.
    • Remove from the group.

8. Additional Resources

  • For more information on User Roles and Descriptions, refer to the written guide linked below the video.
