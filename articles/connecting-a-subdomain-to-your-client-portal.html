Connecting a subdomain to your client portal

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Connecting a subdomain to your client portal allows you to create a clean, branded URL for client portal even if you have your website hosted somewhere else

Instead of using your default url, you can publish your portal on something like:

  • members.yourdomain.com
  • portal.yourdomain.com
  • clients.yourdomain.com

    • This strengthens brand credibility and keeps your ecosystem cohesive without having to purchase another domain name. Keep in mind that this sub domain for the client portal may be used for people who are not actually patients yet but are accessing courses or freebies that are apart of your funnels.

     

    Before You Begin

    Make sure:

  • You own the main domain (e.g. yourdomain.com)
  • You have access to your domain provider (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, etc.)

    • Step 1: Decide on Your Subdomain

    A subdomain is the part that appears before your main domain.

    Example:

    members.yourdomain.com
    “members” is the subdomain.

    Choose something that reflects the purpose of the funnel:

  • members
  • portal
  • join
  • workwith

    • Keep it simple and easy to type.

    Step 2: Add the Subdomain Inside Alvéya

    The process for this is almost identical how you would connect your primary domain so you can watch the video above. Instead of adding the "@", type in your subdomain word.

    1. Go to Settings → Domains inside your sub-account
    2. Click Connect Domain
    3. Enter your chosen subdomain (e.g. members.yourdomain.com)
    4. Continue to the DNS instructions
    5. Alvéya will generate the required DNS record.

      6. Step 3: Update DNS Records in Your Domain Provider

    6. Log into your domain provider.
    7. Go to:
      DNS Settings or Manage DNS
    8. You will add a new record.Most commonly this will be:Type: CNAME
      Host/Name: members (or your chosen subdomain)
      Points to: the target value provided by Alvéya (usually sites.ludicrous.cloud)

      9. Save the record.

      Important:
      Do not modify your main domain A record if your website is hosted elsewhere.

      Only add the new subdomain record.

      Step 4: Verify the Domain in Alvéya

      Return to Alvéya:

      Settings → Domains

      Click Verify.

      DNS propagation can take:

    9. A few minutes
    10. Up to 24 hours in some cases

      11. Once verified, the status will show as connected.

      Step 5: Assign the Subdomain to Your Funnel

      The next screen will be where you can allocate the subdomain to the client portal.

      Your portal will now publish at:

      subdomain.yourdomain.com

      Best Practice Recommendations

      Use:

    11. Your main domain for your website
    12. A dedicated subdomain for funnels
    13. A separate subdomain for email sending
    14. A separate subdomain for client portals

      15. Keeping domains structured prevents conflicts and protects email deliverability.
