Onboarding with Alvéya™

Updated June 2026
Back to Getting Started







Welcome to Alvéya™ knowledge base

your all-in-one system for running a thriving health practice or service-based business.

We understand tech can be overwhelming when it's not your area of genius. After all, you're a health or wellness prac, not a web/tech expert.

Our core mission for Alvéya™ has always been to create a platform that is as user friendly as possible, but to also guide you through the parts that can be a little more in depth.

This checklist will walk you through the essential first steps to get your account ready. Follow along in order and by the end, you’ll have everything connected, branded, and working smoothly.

Step 1: Watch the Welcome Video


Start with a short walkthrough that introduces your dashboard, shows where everything lives, and gives you a feel for the platform.

Step 2: Add Your Business Information


Your business details appear in invoices, emails, and booking pages. Setting these up correctly keeps your communications professional and consistent.

  • Go to Settings → Business Profile
  • Enter your business name, ABN (if applicable), and contact details
  • Upload your logo




📖 How to add your business details →

Step 3: Connect Your Domain


Using your own domain makes everything client-facing look polished and trustworthy.

  • Update your DNS records with your domain provider (A Record + CNAME)
  • Enable SSL for security
  • Test the connection


📖 How to connect your domain →

Step 4: Set Up Email & SMS


Reliable communication is key. Connect your email and SMS so reminders, campaigns, and confirmations send automatically.

  • Add your sending email (SMTP, Gmail, Outlook, or Mailgun)
  • Connect or purchase an SMS number
  • Send a test message


📖 How to connect your email →


📖 How to set up SMS →

Step 5: Create Your Booking Calendar


Make it easy for clients to book with you online.

  • Go to Calendars → Create Calendar
  • Add your availability and appointment types
  • Sync with your external calendar
  • Share your booking link or embed it on your site


📖 How to create your calendar →

Step 6: Import Your Clients


Keep your client database in one place for easier communication and tracking.

  • Go to Contacts → Import
  • Upload a CSV or add contacts manually
  • Apply tags or assign them to a pipeline stage


📖 How to import contacts →

Step 7: Customise Your Branding


Bring your brand to life inside Alvéya™.

  • Set your brand colours and fonts
  • Add your logo to the client portal
  • Customise your email templates


📖 How to update your branding →

Step 8: Download the Mobile App


Manage your bookings, messages, and client updates on the go.

  • Download from Apple App Store or Google Play
  • Log in with your The Alvéya™ details

Step 9: Keep Building


Your account is ready to go 🎉. Now it’s time to build on these foundations. You don’t need to learn everything at once — just choose one area to focus on next.

✨ Common next steps:

  • Launch your first funnel
  • Automate a welcome email sequence
  • Set up payments and invoicing
  • Create your first course or membership
