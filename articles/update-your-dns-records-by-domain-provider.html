Update Your DNS Records (by Domain Provider)

Updated June 2026
Back to Getting Started

When you connect your domain to Alvéya™, you’ll need to update something called your DNS records. This tells the internet where your website, booking pages, and funnels should live.

Because every domain provider looks a little different, we’ve created step-by-step instructions for the most common ones.

Step 1: Add Your Domain in Alvéya™

Before you make changes with your provider, be sure to first add your domain inside Alvéya™.

📖 How to Add a Domain in Alvéya™ →

Step 2: Choose Your Provider

    1. Click the link below for detailed instructions tailored to your domain host:

      • 📖 GoDaddy DNS Instructions
      • 📖 SiteGround DNS Instructions
      • 📖 VentraIP DNS Instructions
      • 📖 Namecheap DNS Instructions
      • 📖 Google Domains DNS Instructions

👉 If you use a different provider, don’t worry — the process is very similar. You’ll just need to update two types of records:

  • A Record → points your root domain (yourclinic.com) to Alvéya™.
  • CNAME Record → points www to Alvéya™.

📖 Generic DNS Instructions (any provider) →

Step 3: Verify in Alvéya™

  1. Once you’ve updated your DNS:

    1. Return to Settings → Domains in Alvéya™.
    2. Click Verify.
    3. Switch on SSL for a secure (https://) connection.

    📖 How to Enable SSL in Alvéya™ →

Troubleshooting

  • DNS changes can take up to 24–48 hours to go live.
  • Use WhatsMyDNS.net to check if your records are updating worldwide.

Double-check that you’ve replaced any old A Records and pointed your www CNAME to Alvéya™.

Still Stuck?

  • Check our DNS Troubleshooting Guide.
  • Use the chat widget inside Alvéya™ for live support.
  • Or contact your domain provider for DNS-specific help.
