How to Cancel Your Alvéya™ Subscription

Updated June 2026
Back to Account & Billing

We understand that sometimes your needs change, and you may be considering cancelling your Alvéya subscription.

Before you do, we’d love to make sure you’re fully supported and that you’re getting the most out of the platform.

 

Need Help Before You Cancel?

If something isn’t working the way you expected, or you’re feeling stuck, we’re here to help.

Many common concerns can be resolved quickly, such as:

• Setting up your funnels, automations or client portal
• Connecting your domain or email settings
• Understanding how features work together
• Simplifying your setup so it feels more manageable

Our support team can guide you through this or even assist with setup where needed.

If you’d like support before cancelling, simply reach out via the chat widget or email us and we’ll help you find a solution.

How to Cancel Your Subscription

If you would still like to proceed, you can cancel your subscription directly from your account.

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Select Billing
  3. Locate your active subscription
  4. Click Cancel Subscription
  5. Follow the prompts to confirm your cancellation

Once cancelled, your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

Important Things to Know

• Your account will remain accessible until the end of your billing cycle
• After cancellation, access to your account and data may be removed
• Any active automations, funnels or services will stop once your account is no longer active

If you’re unsure about anything before cancelling, we recommend reaching out so we can help you make the best decision for your business.
