Communities Playbook

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

A Complete Setup Guide for Alvéya™

The Communities feature allows you to bring your clients, members or audience into one central space inside your Client Portal.

When set up properly, your community can support your programs, strengthen client relationships and create a more connected experience around your work.

This guide will walk you through how to set up your community so it feels aligned, easy to manage and simple for your clients to use.

Each step is linked out to the individual guide and will open in a new window. Keep this master playbook open to comeback to when you finish each step

FAQs

Why am I being asked to create a password when logging in?

This is the standard client login experience. Your access level is determined by your user permissions, not by creating a password.

Will creating a login make me a member instead of an admin?

No. If you have been added as an admin, you will still have admin access once logged in.

Can I control who sees my community?

No. If you have been added as an admin, you will still have admin access once logged in.

Can I connect my community to a course or program?

Yes. Communities can be used alongside courses, memberships or offers inside your Client Portal.

Do my clients need a separate login?

No. Your Client Portal gives them access to everything in one place, including communities, courses and resources.
