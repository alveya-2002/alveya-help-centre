Create a community in Alvéya

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

This article is step one from the Communities playbook. To continue the process of setting up your community after this step, head back to the playbook here.

Written step by step instructions and links to the templates for this video are below.

Written Instructions:

Go to:

Members & Courses → Communities → Groups

Click:

Create Your First Community

Enter the basic details for your group.

You will be able to set:

• Community name
• URL slug (the final part of the link)
• Group description
• Privacy settings

For example, your community URL may look like:

 

www.yourdomain.com/community-name

 

If you want the group to be private, toggle the privacy setting so members must log in to access it.

Add Community Branding

Next you can add the visual branding for your community.

You will be able to upload:

• Community cover image
• Community logo
• Favicon

The favicon is the small icon that appears in the browser tab.

Recommended image sizes are shown inside the upload areas. You can create these graphics easily in tools like Canva.

Logo & Favicon template

Cover Image Template

Once everything is added, click Create Group.

Set Your Community Login

After creating your group, you will be prompted to set your community login password. This is normal and will automatically set your owner/admin permissions

Once your password is set:

  1. Click the confirmation button

  2. Create your profile

  3. Upload your profile photo

Your profile will be visible to other members inside the community.

Review the Community Layout

Once your profile is created, you will see the front end of your community.

The layout includes:

• Cover image at the top of the community
• Community logo
• Navigation sections

You will now be able to customise and manage the community from the backend.
