CRM Playbook

Updated June 2026

A Complete Setup Guide for Alvéya™

Your CRM is the operational heart of Alvéya.

It stores every contact, tracks every interaction, and gives you a complete view of your client journey in one place. When configured properly, your CRM removes manual admin, reduces missed follow-ups, and keeps your practice organised as you grow.

This Playbook walks you through how to structure, clean, and confidently use your CRM from day one.

What You’ll Complete in This Guide

  1. Understand the CRM structure
  2. Import and organise contacts
  3. Set up tags and smart lists
  4. Clean and merge duplicates
  5. Configure contact management tools
  6. Navigate and use the contact file

Understanding the CRM

The CRM is your central client database.

It contains:

  • Contact records
  • Communication history
  • Appointments
  • Tasks
  • Payments
  • Form submissions
  • Course access
  • Activity tracking

It is different from your “Contacts” view only in perspective.

Contacts is the list.
CRM is the system that manages the relationships.

Every interaction feeds into the CRM automatically once your systems are connected.

Steps to setting up your contacts & CRM

Step 1 – Import Your Contacts

If you are migrating from another platform, import your contact list first.

Go to:
Contacts → Import

Upload a CSV file and map fields correctly.

Check:

  • Email fields are matched correctly
  • Phone numbers include country code
  • Tags are assigned if needed

Clean imports prevent future confusion.

Step 2 – Create Tags and Smart Lists

Tags allow you to segment contacts.

Examples:

  • New Lead
  • Active Client
  • Completed Program
  • Webinar Attendee

Smart Lists allow you to dynamically group contacts based on filters such as:

  • Has tag
  • Booked appointment
  • Opened email
  • Pipeline stage

Tags organise.
Smart Lists automate segmentation.

  Learn more Creating tags and building a Smart List.

Step 3 – Merge Duplicate Contacts

Duplicate contacts can occur when:

  • Someone submits multiple forms
  • You import overlapping lists
  • Email and phone entries differ

To merge duplicates:

  1. Search the contact

  2. Select duplicate records
  3. Click “More”
  4. Select “Merge”

Always review before confirming a merge.

Step 4 – Manage Your CRM Structure

Now configure your CRM to match how your business operates.

You may need to:

  • Create custom fields

  • Create pipelines
  • Assign contact owners
  • Define lifecycle stages

This ensures:

  • Clear visibility
  • Proper automation triggers
  • Team accountability

Step 5 – Using the Contact Profile

Every contact file contains a full interaction history.

Inside a contact profile, you can view:

  • All conversations (Email, SMS, Instagram, Facebook)
  • Tags and custom fields
  • Form submissions
  • Booked or cancelled appointments
  • Courses or community access
  • Uploaded documents
  • Payment history
  • Activity logs

From within the contact profile, you can also:

  • Send email or SMS
  • Send Instagram DMs (if connected)
  • Use templates and snippets
  • Leave internal notes
  • Assign tasks
  • Add or remove tags

This becomes your single source of truth.
