Create and manage physical and digital products

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Read written instructions here

Key Steps

1. Accessing the Products Page

  • Navigate to the ‘Payments’ section.
  • Click on ‘Products’ to access the products page.

2. Creating a New Product

  • Click on ‘Create Product’.

  • Enter the product title. If it’s a digital download, specify this in the title.

    3. Adding Product Details

    Add a description using relevant text.

  • Edit the text formatting (font size, style) as needed.

    4. Creating Collections

    If applicable, create collections for products (e.g., ebooks) from the same screen.

    5. Setting Tax Options

    Choose whether to include tax based on global settings.

    6. Additional Product Options

    Add a mockup image for the product.

  • Set the price type (one-time or recurring) and specify the amount and currency.

    7. Setting Compare Price

    If the product is on sale, enter the original price to display a compare price.

    8. Inventory Tracking

    For digital products, inventory tracking is not necessary.

    9. Adding Variants

    If applicable, add multiple prices for different variants (e.g., colors).

    10. SEO Optimization

    Set the SEO title (50-60 characters recommended).

  • Write a description (150-160 characters recommended).

    11. Creating a Custom Handle

    Create a custom handle for the product (e.g., product/product-name).

    12. Saving the Product

    Click ‘Save’ to save the product details.

    13. Editing Product Type

    If the product type is incorrect, go to ‘Actions’ > ‘Edit’.

  • Change the product type to ‘Digital’ under pricing settings.

    14. Uploading Digital Product

    In the ‘Shipping and Delivery’ section, upload the digital product file.

    15. Final Save

    Click ‘Save’ to finalize the product creation.

    Cautionary Notes

    Ensure all required fields are filled out before saving the product.

  • Double-check the product type to avoid confusion between physical and digital products.

    Tips for Efficiency

    Use templates for product descriptions to save time.

  • Keep SEO titles and descriptions ready in advance to streamline the process.
