Set Up an Online Store in Alvéya™

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Your Alvéya™ Online Store lets you sell products and services directly from your website — from professional offerings to physical or digital items.

It’s designed to make setup simple while keeping everything centralised inside your dashboard — from product management and payments to order fulfilment.

Step 1: Create and Configure Your Products

  1. In your dashboard, go to Payments → Products.
  2. Click + Add Product.
  3. Enter your product name, price, and description.
  4. Upload images or videos to showcase your product.
  5. (Optional) Add variants such as size, colour, or material.
  6. Toggle Show in Online Store to make the product visible to customers.

💡 Products won’t appear in your store unless this toggle is turned on.

Step 2: Activate and Customise Your Online Store

You can add your store to a new or existing website.

  1. Go to Sites → Websites.
  2. Click Edit beside your website (or create a new one).
  3. In the Website Builder, click the + icon and choose Online Store.

When added, Alvéya™ automatically creates five store pages for you:

PagePurpose
Products ListDisplays all your available products.
Product DetailsShows the full description, images, and pricing.
Cart PageDisplays the customer’s selected items before checkout.
Checkout PageCollects shipping, coupon, and payment details.
Thank You PageShows the order confirmation after purchase.

💡 You can format each page to match your branding using fonts, colours, and layout adjustments.

Customising store pages for your brand

You can edit the layout, colours, and fonts of each default page using the same controls as your website builder.
Each element includes General and Advanced settings for precise formatting.

Try adding:

  • A header banner for promotions or sales
  • Testimonials or social proof below the products
  • Custom text blocks for shipping or returns information

Step 3: Track Orders and Payments

You can track every sale and transaction from within your Alvéya™ dashboard.

  1. Go to Payments → Orders to view customer orders.
  2. Go to Payments → Transactions to view payments, refunds, and receipts.
  3. Click into any order to see:

    • Order details

    • Customer contact information
    • Payment confirmation and totals

💡 You can also process refunds directly from the Transactions area.

Automating order and payment tracking

Use these built-in automations to simplify your workflow:

  • Order Submitted trigger: Sends automated email confirmations.
  • Payment Received trigger: Sends receipts or notifications to your team.
  • Shopping Cart element: Auto-populates order details inside your email templates.

These triggers are available in Automations → Workflows.

Step 4: Fulfil Orders and Add Shipment Details

All orders appear as Unfulfilled until they’re marked complete.

  1. Go to Payments → Orders and open the relevant order.
  2. Click Fulfil Items.
  3. Add your tracking number, shipping provider, and tracking URL.
  4. Save your changes — the order will be updated to Fulfilled.

💡 Customers can be notified with tracking details automatically once fulfilment notifications are enabled in your account.

How fulfilment tracking works

When you mark an order as fulfilled, Alvéya™ automatically records:

  • Fulfilment date
  • Items fulfilled
  • Tracking information (number, provider, and URL)

You can review all fulfilment history within the order details at any time.

Additional Notes

  • Only one-time products appear in your online store. Use Funnels or Order Forms to sell recurring or subscription products.
  • Coupon codes, receipts, and payment triggers work automatically.
  • Existing products are not shown by default — use the toggle to display them in your store.
  • Orders, payments, and refunds can all be managed from Payments → Orders and Payments → Transactions.

FAQS

Can I sell recurring products in my online store?
Only one-time products can be added to the online store. If you want to sell recurring or subscription-based products, you can still do this using funnels or order forms instead.

I created a test store. How do I delete it?
To delete a store, simply remove any of the automatically generated store pages (such as the Products page, Cart page, Checkout page, or Thank You page). When you confirm the deletion, all store data linked to those pages will also be removed.

Can I set different prices for product variants?
Yes. Each variant can have its own price. By default, variants inherit the main product price, but you can override this and set unique pricing for each variant.

How do I mark orders as fulfilled and share shipment details?
You can fulfil an order by going to Payments → Orders, opening the order, and selecting “Fulfil Items.” Here you can add the tracking number, shipping provider, and tracking URL. When saved, these fulfilment details are recorded in the order history. If fulfilment notifications are available in your account, customers will receive tracking updates automatically.

Can I create multiple stores within the same location?
Yes. You can create more than one store inside a single Alvéya™ location. Each store can have different products, layouts, and pages depending on your needs.
