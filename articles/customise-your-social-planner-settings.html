Customise your Social Planner Settings

Updated June 2026
Back to Automations

Manage connections, permissions, notifications and branding for your social content.

The Social Planner Settings area allows you to control how your accounts connect, how posts are published, and how your content is branded across platforms. This page walks through each tab so you can configure your system correctly from the beginning.

This Playbook walks you through the essential setup so you can start scheduling confidently.

Part 1 Connect Facebook through Alvéya Settings - > Integrations

Step 1: Go to Settings > Integrations

In your Alvéya dashboard, navigate to Settings and scroll down to Integrations.

Note: Even if you have already connected your social media through the Social Planner, you still need to complete this integration step separately.

Step 2: Click Connect on Facebook

Locate the Facebook option and click the Connect button. Because Facebook manages Instagram, this single connection covers both platforms.

Step 3: Log in and grant permissions

You will be prompted to log in to Facebook and give Lead Connector permission to communicate directly through Facebook. Follow the prompts and select your account (e.g. Alvéya). Choose All Leads, then click Connect and Continue.

Step 4: Skip the lead capture form (optional)

The next screen asks about lead capture forms within Facebook. If you are only using this connection for automations — not Facebook lead ads — you can select No, I will do it later and move on.

Part 2: Connect Facebook & Instagram in the social planner settings

Following the steps below, you will be able to connect your Facebook pages with the platform.

1. If you have already connected your Facebook pages from any profile previously and want to add some new pages, you will have to ‘Remove’ the application from your Facebook Settings >  Business Integration section on your Facebook account and follow the steps below all over again as demonstrated in the image below. 

2. To add all your Facebook pages, it is necessary that you have Admin rights. It is mandatory for you to log yourself in with the same profile that has all the pages in which you are an admin.

Due to Facebook’s recent API changes, it will no longer promote posting to Facebook personal profiles, however, you can add pages from different profiles for Social Planner.

 

Step 1. Go to Social Planner > Setting

To get started, please go to Social Planner. In the top right corner of Social Planner, there is a Setting option (Gear icon). Please navigate to Settings by clicking it.

Step 2. Click on Connect a new page, Facebook Page

The next step is to click on Connect a new Facebook page of the table as shown below to manage multiple Facebook Pages.

Step 3. Give permissions to fetch Facebook page(s)

If you’re connecting any profile for the first time, you will have to make sure you give all the permissions to it. Please click Continue to complete the authorization process for fetching the Facebook page(s).

Step 4. Select Facebook Page(s) to connect

It will list all the Facebook pages for this profile, select the list of the ones you wish to connect for Social Planner.

Step 5. Verify your connection

Last but not least, all of the selected pages in the previous step will be listed down showing a successful connection; as shown in the image below. 

Following these instructions, you can add as many pages as you wish from the same or different Facebook profiles.

Troubleshooting connections - Fix Missing Permissions in Meta Business Suite

If you see a Missing Permissions error after connecting, you need to grant Lead Connector access directly in Meta Business Suite. Here is how:

Step 1: Open Meta Business Suite

Go to Meta Business Suite and select your business page (e.g. Alvéya) from the profile selector.

Step 2: Navigate to Settings > Connected Apps

In the left-hand menu, go to Settings, then scroll down until you find the Connected Apps or Integrations section. Locate Lead Connector in the list.

Step 3: Set permissions to Allow All Conversations

Click Manage next to Lead Connector, then select Allow All Conversations. This grants Lead Connector permission to view and respond to conversations, even when they are not actively routed through another app — which is required for automations to work correctly.

Step 8: Return to Alvéya and refresh

Go back to your Alvéya dashboard. Refresh the page and navigate to your automation triggers. The Facebook/Instagram trigger should now display as active (shown in green). You can now select your connected page or account from the dropdown.

Push Notifications for Scheduled posts or stories

Settings > Social Planner > Notifications

The Notifications tab lets you configure automated email alerts for key social media events — so you and your team are always informed when something needs attention.

What You Can Be Notified About

You can set up notifications for three types of events:

  • Post approvals — when a scheduled post is approved and ready to go
  • Post failures — when a post fails to publish so it can be addressed quickly
  • Account expirations — when a connected social account token is about to expire and needs refreshing

Notification Settings at a Glance

Each notification rule has the following fields:

Field

What it does

Notification Type

The trigger for the alert — choose from post approval, post failure, or account pre-expiry.

Email Template

The email template used for the notification. You can customise this in your email templates settings.

Users to Notify

Select which team members should receive the alert. You can add multiple users.

Reminder Frequency

How often the reminder is resent if the issue has not been resolved — useful for expiry alerts.

Status

Toggle the notification rule on or off without deleting it.

Account Pre-Expiry Notifications

This is the most important notification to have active. Social media platforms periodically require you to re-authenticate your connected accounts — if the token expires, your scheduled posts will stop publishing.

The Account Pre-Expiry alert sends an email before your token expires, giving you time to reconnect the account and avoid any disruption to your content schedule.

Tip: We recommend enabling this notification for all team members who manage social media, and setting a reminder frequency so the alert repeats until the account has been refreshed.

Social Categories

Social Categories allow you to organise your content by theme.

Examples:

  • Educational
  • Engament
  • Promotions
  • Behind the Scenes
  • Inspiring/Empowering
  • Email List Growth/Opt In Promotion

Using categories helps you:

  • Maintain content balance
  • Track strategy patterns
  • Filter content inside the planner

To create a category:

  1. Click Add Category
  2. Name the category
  3. Save

You can edit or remove categories at any time.

Watermark

The Watermark tab allows you to apply branded overlays to your visual content.

You can:

  • Upload watermark images
  • Assign a different watermark per social account
  • Update watermark versions

This ensures:

  • Consistent brand presence
  • Subtle ownership of visual assets
  • Platform-specific branding where required

Note: Posts created before updating your watermark will retain their original watermark. To apply a new one, you must edit and resave the post.

Global Settings

Global Settings control how media behaves across all social posts.

Here you can manage:

Image Optimisation

Controls how images are compressed before publishing.

  • Enabled: Faster load time, slightly reduced image quality
  • Disabled: Higher quality, larger file size

If brand visuals are critical, consider managing optimisation carefully.

Apply Watermark by Default

If enabled, new posts will automatically use the latest assigned watermark.

If disabled, watermarks must be applied manually when creating posts.

Best Practice Setup

For a stable system:

  • Connect all required social accounts first

  • Create your core content categories
  • Assign watermarks per platform
  • Confirm timezone in Global Settings

Once configured, your Social Planner will run smoothly with minimal maintenance.
