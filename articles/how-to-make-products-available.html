How to Make Products Available

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Once you have created your product inside Alvéya, the next step is making it available for purchase.

Creating a product does not automatically make it visible to your audience. You will need to connect it to a online store, sales page, order form or funnel so people can actually access and buy it.

This lesson will walk you through the different ways to make your products available.

Step 1 — Check Your Product Is Set Up Correctly

Before making your product available, make sure it is fully set up.

Check that you have:

• Added a product name and description
• Set your pricing (one-time, subscription or payment plan)
• Connected your payment gateway (such as Stripe)
• Saved and published the product

Once this is complete, you are ready to connect it to a page.

Step 2 — Choose How You Want to Sell Your Product

There are a few different ways to make your product available depending on how you want to sell it.

You can:

• Add it to a sales page with a checkout button
• Connect it to an order form
• Include it inside a funnel
• Link directly to a checkout page
• Add it to your shop page

Most users will use a funnel or sales page for the best results.

Option 1 — Add Product to an Order Form

Order forms are the most common way to sell products in Alvéya.

  1. Go to Sites → Forms
  2. Create or edit an existing form
  3. Add the Order / Payment element
  4. Select your product from the product list
  5. Save and publish the form

This form can now be embedded or linked anywhere.

Option 2 — Add Product to a Funnel or Page

You can also connect your product to a funnel or landing page.

  1. Go to Sites → Funnels
  2. Open your funnel or create a new one
  3. Add or edit a page (such as a sales page or checkout page)
  4. Add a button or order form element
  5. Link it to your product

This allows you to guide users through a full journey:

Sales page → checkout → confirmation → delivery

Option 3 — Add Product to Your Shop

If you are using the shop feature, you can display your product there.

  1. Go to Payments → Products
  2. Ensure your product is active
  3. Add it to your shop page or product list

This allows customers to browse and purchase multiple products in one place.

Step 3 — Share Your Product Link

Once your product is connected to a page or form, you can share it with your audience.

You can:

• Add it to your website
• Share it via email campaigns
• Link it in your social media
• Include it in your client portal
• Use it inside webinars or funnels

Step 4 — Test Your Checkout

Before promoting your product, test the full process.

Check that:

• The product displays correctly
• The checkout works
• Payments process successfully
• Confirmation emails are sent
• Any automations or access are triggered

Testing ensures a smooth experience for your customers.

Important to Know

Products are not automatically visible until they are connected to a page, form or funnel.

Think of your product as the backend setup, and your pages and funnels as the front-facing experience your customers interact with.

Next Step

Once your product is available, you can:

• Automate delivery using workflows
• Tag customers for segmentation
• Connect products to courses or memberships
• Track sales and performance

This allows you to create a seamless and scalable sales system inside Alvéya.
