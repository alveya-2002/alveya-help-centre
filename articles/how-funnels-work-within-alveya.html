How funnels work within Alvéya

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

In this video, I walk you through how to use the Funnels feature in Alvear, whether you have a website with us or externally. I explain the concept of a funnel, which is a step-by-step process that can include opt-ins, sales pages, and confirmation pages. I also touch on the importance of A-B testing and customising your sales page to fit your needs. If you’re setting up a funnel, remember to add your product before connecting it to the order form. I encourage you to check out the other tutorial for more details on customising your pages.

1. Understanding Funnels

  • The Funnels feature in Alvear can be used with your existing website (e.g., Squarespace, WordPress, Wix) or independently.
  • A funnel is a step-by-step process for guiding users through actions like signing up or making a purchase.

 2. Creating a New Funnel

  • Click on 'New Funnel' to start.
  • Choose to create from a blank template or select from existing templates.

 3. Funnel Structure Example

  • A typical funnel may include:
    • Step 1: Free opt-in
    • Step 2: Sales page
    • Step 3: Sign-up page
    • Step 4: Sign-up confirmation page

  • You can customize the steps based on your needs.

 4. A/B Testing

  • The funnel feature allows for A/B testing to compare different versions of sales pages.

 5. Editing the Sales Page

  • Customize colors, copy, images, and layout of the sales page.
  • You can rearrange, delete, or duplicate sections as needed.

 6. Setting Up Call-to-Action Buttons

  • Each call-to-action button links to the next step in the funnel.
  • Ensure buttons are set to redirect users correctly (e.g., to the sign-up page).

 7. Adding Products to the Funnel

  • To use an order form, you must first add your product to the funnel.
  • This is done before editing the sales page.

 8. Confirmation Page Setup

  • After checkout, users should be directed to a confirmation page.
  • This page should inform users about what to expect next (e.g., checking email for confirmation).

9. Creating an Opt-in Funnel

  • For an opt-in funnel, create a form in the form builder first.
  • Add the form to the sales page or replace the order form with the opt-in form.

 10. Additional Resources

  • Watch other videos for setting up automation and forms.
  • Good luck with your funnel setup!
