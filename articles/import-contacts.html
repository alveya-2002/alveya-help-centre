How to Import Contacts from Another Email Campaign Platform

Updated June 2026
Back to CRM & Contacts

What You'll Need

  • Your contact list exported as a CSV file (Excel spreadsheet in CSV format) from your previous email platform

Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Navigate to Contacts Go to the Contacts section in the platform.

Step 2: Start the Import Click the Import button in the top right corner.

Step 3: Choose Import Type Select the Contacts option (it should be pre-selected) and click Next.

Step 4: Prepare Your File

  • If you're unsure of the required format, download the sample file provided on this screen as a reference.
  • Export your contacts from your previous email platform in the same format.

    • Step 5: Upload Your File Drag and drop your CSV file into the upload area.

    Step 6: Choose How to Import Select one of the following options based on your needs:

  • Create new contacts – adds all contacts as new entries
  • Create and update contacts – adds new contacts and updates any that already exist
  • Update contacts only – only updates existing contacts, no new ones created

    • Click Next.

    Step 7: Map Your Fields Match the columns from your spreadsheet to the correct fields in the platform (e.g. phone → phone, email → email, first name, last name, business name, etc.). If you don't want a field to be updated, tick the "do not update this value" option. Click Next.

    Step 8: Configure Additional Options On this screen you can optionally:

  • Add imported contacts to an existing or new smart list / segmented list
  • Add contacts into a workflow
  • Add tags to the imported contacts

    • Review all the details carefully before proceeding.

    Step 9: Confirm and Start Import Once everything looks correct, tick "I confirm" and click Start Bulk Import. The import may take some time to process.

    Step 10: Check Import Progress Click on Bulk Actions to monitor the progress of your import. From here you can also:

  • Revert the import if you made a mistake
  • View details to see exactly what was imported

    • Once complete, refresh your screen and your new contacts will appear.

    💡 Tip: Always download and check the sample file first to make sure your CSV is formatted correctly before importing.
