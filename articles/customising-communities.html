Customising Individual Communities: Colours & Settings

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Within Alvéya, you can customise the look of each community group.Each community can have a unique appearance aligned with your color palette.A new community will initially have standard blue buttons and grey backgrounds.

Watch the video below to learn how to customise each community group.

Written Instructions:

Accessing Community Settings

  • Log in to the community group you created.

  • Navigate to the ‘Settings’ section on the bottom wright side of the screen.

Modifying Basic Settings

  • Change the group name and adjust the URL.

  • If applicable, go back and set up a subdomain for your client portal which will then change the community group link.

  • Adjust the group description and privacy settings if needed (public/private).

Subscription and Newsletter Options

  • Set up a paid subscription for the community group if desired.

  • Enable newsletter options to summarise posts or member activity.

Branding Customisation

  • Update the favicon, community cover image, and logo.

  • Change button colors under the ‘Themes’ section.

Theme Customisation

  • Choose from existing community themes or customise your own.

  • Keep dark colors darker and light colors lighter from your palette.

  • Recommended to keep error messages in red for visibility.

Membership Management

  • Decide if members can see tabs for learning, events, leaderboard, etc.

  • Add membership questions for access requests (e.g., email address tracking).

Gamification Setup

  • Set up gamification features for member engagement.

  • Define levels and rewards for completing modules.

Adding Promotional Links

  • Include any promotional or affiliate links relevant to your community.

Content Reporting and Member Importing

  • View reported content in the designated section.

  • Import Skool group members and their content if needed.

After customisation, the community will reflect your brand identity, contrasting the initial bland appearance.
