Connecting Instagram and Facebook for Automations

Updated June 2026
Back to Integrations

This guide outlines the steps to connect your Instagram and Facebook accounts using Lead Connector for automation purposes.

Written step by step instructions and links to the templates for this video are below.

https://youtu.be/sEL-k7_TzCw

WRITTEN INSTRUCTIONS:

  • Ensure you have the correct permissions set in both Facebook and Instagram to avoid integration issues.

  • If you encounter errors, double-check the connection settings in MetaBusiness Suite.

### Cautionary Notes

Key Steps

Step 1: Access Integrations

  • Go to your settings.

  • Scroll down to the ‘Integrations’ section.

Step 2: Connect Facebook

  • Click on the ‘Connect’ button for Facebook.

  • Note: Even if connected through the Social Planner, this step is necessary.

Step 3: Log In to Facebook

  • Log in to your Facebook account.

  • Grant permission to Lead Connector to access your Facebook account.

Step 4: Select Profile and Permissions

  • Choose the profile you want to connect (e.g., Alvear).

  • Select ‘All Leads’ and click ‘Connect and Continue’.

Step 5: Optional Lead Capture Forms

  • If you want to use lead capture forms, proceed with the setup.

  • If not, select ‘No, I will do it later’.

Step 6: Manage Permissions in MetaBusiness Suite

  • Go to MetaBusiness Suite.

  • Select your profile (e.g., Alvear).

  • Navigate to ‘Settings’ > ‘Lead Connector’ > ‘Manage’.

  • Allow ‘All Conversations’.

Step 7: Refresh and Start New Trigger

  • Refresh the page.

  • Start a new trigger and ensure it is active (green indicator).

Step 8: Check Instagram Connection

  • In MetaBusiness Suite, go to ‘Pages’ > ‘Connected Accounts’.

  • Ensure your Instagram account is listed.

Step 9: Verify Link Accounts in Facebook Settings

  • On your Facebook page, go to ‘Settings’.

  • Scroll to ‘Link Accounts’ and verify Instagram connection.

Step 10: Check Instagram Settings

  • Open Instagram app.

  • Go to ‘Menu’ > ‘Messages’ > ‘Stories Reply’.

  • Ensure the toggle for ‘Allow Access to Messages’ is on.

### Tips for Efficiency

  • Regularly check your connections to ensure they remain active.

  • Familiarise yourself with the MetaBusiness Suite interface to streamline the process.

  • Ensure you have the correct permissions set in both Facebook and Instagram to avoid integration issues.

  • If you encounter errors, double-check the connection settings in MetaBusiness Suite.
