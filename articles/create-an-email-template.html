Create an email template

Updated June 2026
Back to Getting Started

Creating your own email templates in Alvéya™ helps you save time and keep your communications consistent across automations, appointment reminders, and campaigns. You can use templates for confirmations, cancellations, newsletters, and more.

Step 1: Go to the Email Template Library

  1. From your Alvéya™ dashboard, click Marketing → Emails → Templates.
  2. Click + New and select Blank Template.

💡 It’s best to create templates before building your workflows or campaigns so you can reuse them easily across your account.

Step 2: Choose a Template Type

You’ll see two editor options:

  • Plain Text Editor → best for system or appointment emails (simple, reliable formatting).
  • Design Editor → ideal for branded newsletters or promotional emails.

Select the editor that suits your goal, then click Create Template.

Step 3: Add Your Email Content

  1. Paste your email copy into the editor.
  2. Remove any placeholder text that appears.
  3. Rename your template clearly, such as Appointment Confirmation – Plain Text or Newsletter Template.

💡 Keep your subject line short and clear — aim for under 50 characters.

Step 4: Insert Custom Values (Optional)

You can personalise your emails by adding Custom Values — these automatically pull contact or appointment details.

  1. Click the Custom Values icon in the toolbar.
  2. Select a field from the list, such as:
    • Contact → First Name
    • Appointment → Start Date
    • Appointment → Start Time
    • Location → Name

  3. The value will appear in brackets (e.g. {{contact.first_name}}).

💡 Use only the fields you need to keep emails clean and easy to read.

Step 5: Add Reschedule or Cancellation Links

If you’re creating an appointment-related template:

  1. Type text such as “Reschedule Appointment” or “Cancel Appointment.”
  2. Highlight the text and click the Link icon in the toolbar.
  3. Open the Custom Values menu → choose:
    • Appointment → Reschedule Link, or
    • Appointment → Cancel Link

  4. Paste the value into the link URL field.

💡 Including reschedule and cancellation links helps reduce no-shows and support requests.

Step 6: Save and Test Your Template

  1. Click Save Template in the top-right corner.
  2. Use Send Test Email to preview your message and confirm formatting, spacing, and links are correct.

Step 7: Organise Your Templates

To stay organised:

  1. Use folders to group templates (e.g. Appointments, Newsletters, Internal Messages).
  2. Drag and drop each template into the right folder.

💡 Naming templates consistently (e.g. “Appointment Reminder – Email”) makes them easy to find later.
