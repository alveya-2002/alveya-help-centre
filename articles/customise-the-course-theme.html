Customise the course theme

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Navigate to the "Customise" option in your Course Outline.

Click on "Browse System Templates".

You can choose from any of the four templates that are available. You're more looking at the layout over the images and colours as these are all customisable. Select "Neo Classic" Theme to utilise the New Customiser.
Click Apply

You can do modifications as per the available customisation options and then save this as a template for future use or save just for this course

If you choose to Save it, you can later find this under "My Templates" option on the main "Customise" Screen.
