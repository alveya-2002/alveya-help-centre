A Complete Setup Guide for Alvéya™

Your domain setup is the foundation of your entire digital ecosystem.

Inside Alvéya™, domains control how your website loads, how your emails are delivered, how your client portal is accessed, and how your links appear publicly.

This Playbook walks you through every domain type available in Alvéya™, explains when to use each one, and guides you step-by-step through setup.

This Playbook walks you through the essential setup so you can start scheduling confidently.

Domain Types Explained

Main Website Domain (Recommended)

This is your primary public-facing domain.

Example:
yourbusiness.com

Use this for:

  • Your website

  • Funnels

  • Landing pages

  • Blog

  • Online store

If everything is built inside Alvéya™, this domain becomes the base for your ecosystem.

Important:
Your main domain should not be used for email sending or your client portal. Those require separate subdomains.

Dedicated Email Sending Domain (Recommended)

This is a subdomain used only for email delivery.

Example:
mail.yourbusiness.com

Clients will not see this domain directly. It exists to:

  • Improve deliverability

  • Protect your main domain’s reputation

  • Maintain long-term sender health

Using a dedicated sending domain is best practice for professional email marketing.

Client Portal Domain (Optional)

This is a branded subdomain used for your client portal.

Example:
clients.yourbusiness.com
members.yourbusiness.com

This replaces the default system-generated portal link and creates a more professional experience for your clients.

Branded Domain (Optional)

This is a custom subdomain used for:

  • Forms

  • Calendars

  • Proposals

  • Invoices

Example:
book.yourbusiness.com
forms.yourbusiness.com

Instead of using system URLs, you can create a clean branded experience across every touchpoint.

Step 2 – Connect Your Main Domain

Go to:
Settings → Domains

📖 Step-by-step DNS update instructions →

Once DNS is updated, allow time for propagation (can take up to 24 hours, often much faster).

Step 3 – Set Up Your Email Sending Domain

Create a subdomain in your DNS provider.

Example:
mail.yourbusiness.com

Inside Alvéya, connect this as your dedicated email sending domain.

You will be asked to add specific DNS records (usually CNAME and TXT records).

This step is essential for:

  • Authentication

  • SPF

  • DKIM

  • Deliverability health

FAQs

Can I use the same domain for everything?

You should use your main domain for websites and funnels.

However:

  • Email sending must use a separate subdomain

  • Client portal should use a separate subdomain

  • Branded links should use separate subdomains

This protects domain reputation and keeps systems clean.

Can I use a .com.au domain?

Yes.

Some domain extensions cannot be purchased or transferred directly inside certain platforms. In that case:

  1. Purchase the domain through a third-party provider

  2. Update DNS manually (Or if you are with Godaddy or have your domain rerouted through Cloudflare, it can add the DNS records automatically)

  3. Connect inside Alvéya

This works seamlessly once DNS is configured correctly.

Why is my domain not connecting?

Common reasons:

  • DNS records not entered correctly

  • Propagation still in progress

  • CNAME conflicts

  • Domain still connected to another platform

Check spelling carefully and confirm there are no duplicate records.

If you still cannot fix it, contact your domain name host.

Can the Alvéya team connect my domain for me

Due to security risk, we cannot connect your domain for you. Your domain name is a major asset for your business and we do not want to compromise the security of it. Please contact your domain host provider if you require assistance.

How long does DNS propagation take?

Anywhere from a few minutes to 24 hours.

Most updates reflect within 1–2 hours.

Troubleshooting

Issue: Domain shows as pending

Fix: Double-check CNAME target values and remove conflicting A records.

Issue: Emails landing in spam

Fix: Confirm SPF, DKIM and domain authentication records are valid. Check that you dont have multiple SPF records. Sometimes placing your domain in a DNS record checker or secure AI model can help pin point the issues

Issue: Portal link not working

Fix: Ensure subdomain is correctly pointed
