Create a Marketing Email in Alvéya™

Updated June 2026
Back to Email Marketing

Alvéya makes it easy to create and send professional marketing emails using built-in templates and a visual email builder. This guide walks you through creating a standard email campaign, from setup through to sending or scheduling.

Step 1: Navigate to the Email Campaigns Area

From your Alvéya dashboard, go to Marketing, then select Email.
Click Create Campaign to begin setting up a new marketing email.

This is where all one-off email campaigns and broadcasts are managed.

Step 2: Choose an Email Template

Next, select the email template you would like to use for your campaign.
You can preview each template before selecting it to ensure it suits your message and layout needs.

Templates help you get started quickly, but they can be fully customised once you enter the builder.

Step 3: Design Your Email Content

Once a template is selected, you will be taken into the Email Builder.

Here you can:

  • Edit text and headings
  • Add images, buttons, dividers, and spacers
  • Adjust colours and layout to match your brand
  • Rearrange sections using drag and drop

The Email Builder is designed to be flexible, allowing you to work on different sections in any order and review the email as a whole before sending.

Step 4: Prepare Your Email for Sending

When your email design is complete, click Send or Schedule.

Before sending, complete the pre-send checklist to ensure everything is set up correctly.

Pre-Sending Checklist

  • Select recipients in the To section using individual emails or smart lists
  • Choose the sender email address in the From section
  • Add a sender name if desired
  • Write a clear subject line
  • Add preview text if needed

Next, choose how the email will be delivered.

You can select Send Now to send immediately, or Schedule to send at a later date and time. Scheduled emails are sent according to the location time zone.

Managing Scheduled Emails

If an email is scheduled, you will have the option to reschedule or delete it if needed.
Rescheduling is allowed up to one hour before the scheduled send time.

For example, if an email is scheduled for 11:00 AM, it can be rescheduled until 10:00 AM.
