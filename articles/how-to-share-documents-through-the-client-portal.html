How to Share Documents Through the Client Portal

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

This article is step one from the Client portal playbook. To continue the process of setting up your Client portal after this step, head back to the playbook here.

Written step by step instructions and links to the templates for this video are below.

https://youtu.be/LoHD3nOnWqg

WRITTEN INSTRUCTIONS:

Step 1: Access Client Portal Settings

  • Go to Websites and Funnels.

  • Hover over Client Portal.

  • Click on Settings.

Step 2: Enable Document Sharing

  • Navigate to App Permissions.

  • Ensure that Documents is switched on under Shared Files.

Step 3: Invite Your Contact to the Portal

  • If not already done, invite your contact to the portal.

  • Go to Contacts and click on the contact’s name.

Step 4: Add Document for Sharing

  • On the right-hand side, click on Documents.

  • Click on Add.

Step 5: Upload Your Document

  • Upload the document you wish to share.

  • Choose whether to share it on the Client Portal or keep it as an internal document.

Step 6: Confirm Document Upload

  • After uploading, click Upload to confirm.

  • Refresh the page to see the document listed on the right-hand side.

Step 7: View Document Filters

  • You can filter documents by All, Internal, Sent, and Received.

Step 8: Client View of Shared Documents

  • Once the client logs into their Client Portal, they will see Shared Files on the right-hand side.

  • Refresh the page to see the uploaded document appear.

Step 9: Client Uploads Documents

  • Clients can click on Add Files to upload their own documents.
