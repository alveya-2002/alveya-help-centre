Add a user to your Alvéya account

Updated June 2026
Back to Account & Billing

Go to Settings, My staff, Click Add User

Add their personal details, Next

Roles & Permissions:

Admin: full access to all modules and settings in the sub-account

User: restricted access; granular permissions apply

Complete the rest of the set up details.

If you have multiple staff needing a similar role setup, you can copy permissions from another user

Why isn't it allowing me to add a new user?

This message usually appears when the person you are trying to add is already connected to another Alvéya™ account.

Because of the way user permissions work, this can sometimes prevent you from adding them directly from your dashboard.

We can add this person to your account from our end if needed. It is just important to note that if they currently have admin access in another Alvéya™ account, they will also have admin access in your account.

If you would prefer them to have user access only, you will need to ask them for an email address that is not already connected to an Alvéya™ account. You can then add that email address as a team member inside your account.

If you would like us to add them for you, simply send their name and email address through the chat widget, and confirm that you would like them to have access to your account. Our team can then jump in and update this for you.
