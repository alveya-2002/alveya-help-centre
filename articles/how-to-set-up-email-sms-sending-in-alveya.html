How to Set Up Email &amp; SMS Sending in Alvéya™

Updated June 2026
Back to Email Marketing

Alvéya™ lets you send emails and SMS directly from your account so you can confirm bookings, deliver resources, and stay in touch with your clients. Before you start building automations or campaigns, you’ll need to connect your sending email and secure an SMS number.

Step 1: Decide Which Email Option to Use

You can connect your email in one of three ways:

  • SMTP → works with most email providers.
  • Google (Gmail / Google Workspace) → quick if your business email runs through Google.
  • Outlook / Microsoft 365 → for those using Microsoft.
  • Mailgun → good if you want higher deliverability at scale.

Step 2: Connect Your Email

  1. Log into Alvéya™.
  2. Go to Settings → Email Services.
  3. Click Add Service and choose your provider.
  4. Follow the prompts (username, password, SMTP server, port, or OAuth login for Google/Outlook).
  5. Save and test your connection.

Step 3: Purchase or Connect an SMS Number

To send SMS reminders and campaigns, you’ll need a dedicated number.

  1. In Alvéya™, go to Settings → Phone Numbers.
  2. Click Add Number.
  3. Search by country (e.g., Australia) and choose a number.
  4. Click Save.

How to Buy & Verify an SMS Number →

Step 4: Verify Everything Works

  • Send yourself a test email.
  • Send a test SMS.
  • Check that replies show up in the Conversations tab.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Emails not sending? Check SMTP username, password, and port.
  • SMS not sending? Make sure you’ve added credits (if applicable) and that the number is verified.
  • Deliverability issues? Always use your own custom domain for sending — avoid free emails like @gmail.com.
