Setting up your Client Portal

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

This article is step one from the Client Portal playbook. To continue the process of setting up your community after this step, head back to the playbook here.

Written step by step instructions and links to the templates for this video are below.

https://youtu.be/DTHDEFqqmho

Written Instructions

Communities are part of your Client Portal, so the next step is to configure the portal settings.

Go to:

Communities → Settings

Here you will see several sections:

• Domain
• Branding
• Permissions
• Email settings

Connect a Custom Domain (Optional)

You can create a custom domain or subdomain for your portal.

For example:

 

members.yourdomain.com

 

If you would like to do this, follow the Domain Setup Tutorial in the domain module.

Otherwise you can continue using the default Alvéya domain.

Customise Your Client Portal Branding

Inside the Branding section you can customise how your client portal appears.

You can set:

• Portal name
• Portal image (Canva Template)

• Logo & Favicon template
• Support email address
• Business name
• Copyright year

These settings control the overall appearance of the portal your clients will access.

Advanced users can also add custom CSS or JavaScript, but this is optional.

Once completed, click Save Settings.

Configure Portal Permissions

Next you can choose which features appear inside your client portal.

Go to:

Settings → Permissions

Here you can turn portal features on or off, including:

• Communities
• Courses
• Affiliates
• Billing and subscriptions
• Contracts
• Estimates
• File sharing

You can also choose the default landing page for your portal.

For example, members can land on:

• Communities
• Courses
• Affiliates
• Portal home page

Choose the option that best suits your program or membership.

Configure Portal Email Settings

Inside Email Settings you can customise the emails sent through your client portal.

You will find email templates for:

• Client portal notifications
• Course notifications
• Invoices
• Estimates
• Contracts
• Community notifications

Each template includes a default email, which you can edit or replace.

You can also enable or disable specific emails depending on your needs.
