Should you reroute your domain through Cloudflare for ease?

Updated June 2026
Back to Getting Started

Alvéya can automatically add your domain records to Cloudlfare rather than moving them manually yourself. If you are ok to add them yourself, you can skip this video but otherwise, watch along to decide if you should

1. Understanding the Benefits of Cloudflare

  • Cloudflare allows for automatic addition of domain records.
  • It can improve speed and security for your domain.
  • Centralizes management of multiple domain names in one place.

2. Keeping Your Domain Registration

  • Maintain your domain registration with your current provider (e.g., GoDaddy, Crazy Domains).
  • Route your domain through Cloudflare for easier management.

 3. Onboarding Your Domain to Cloudflare

  • Start the onboarding process by selecting your domain (e.g., lharrison.design).
  • Choose to perform a quick scan of existing DNS records (recommended) or enter them manually.

 4. Scanning DNS Records

  • Cloudflare will scan your current DNS records and display them.
  • Review the scanned records for accuracy.

 5. Adjusting Proxied Status

  • Decide whether to keep the proxied status on or off for your records.
  • In this case, turn off the proxied status.

6. Updating Nameservers in Crazy Domains

  • Log into Crazy Domains and navigate to your domain management settings.
  • Click on the cogwheel, select 'manage domain', and go to 'nameservers'.
  • Replace the existing nameservers with the ones provided by Cloudflare.

 7. Saving Changes and Propagation

  • Save the updated nameserver records.
  • Acknowledge that it may take some time for changes to propagate.

 8. Automatic DNS Record Management

  • Once set up, any changes or additions to your domain in Alvear will automatically update DNS records in Cloudflare.
  • This eliminates the need for manual copying and pasting of records.
