Updated June 2026

A Complete Setup Guide for Alvéya™

Your website is your digital foundation. It is where clients learn about your services, understand your approach, and decide whether to book with you.

When set up correctly, your website:

  • Builds credibility and trust
  • Clearly communicates your services
  • Connects seamlessly with bookings, forms, and funnels
  • Supports your SEO and long-term visibility

This Playbook walks you through setting up your website in the correct order so it works properly from day one.

What You’ll Complete in This Guide

You’ll move through a structured setup process:

  1. Create or Select Your Website
  2. Configure Website Settings
  3. Customise Your Pages
  4. Set SEO & Tracking Basics
  5. Connect Your Domain
  6. Test & Publish

Each step includes:

  • Why the step matters
  • Basic click paths
  • Optional short video walkthroughs
  • 📖 Links to detailed support guides where needed

    • Work through each step in order and test as you go.

    Before You Begin

    Before starting, make sure:

  • Your business details are added under Settings
  • Your brand colours and fonts are set up in Brand Boards
  • You know whether you’re replacing an existing website or launching fresh

    • If you’re migrating from another platform, ensure you have access to your domain registrar.
