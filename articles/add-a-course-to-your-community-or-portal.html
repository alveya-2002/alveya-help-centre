Add a Course to Your Community or portal

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

Adding your course to the client portal allows you to:

• Deliver content in one organised space
• Control access based on your program or offer
• Create a seamless experience for your clients

 

Watch the video above to learn how

https://youtu.be/toqB4JnwEwM

Written Instructions:

Step 1 — Open the Client Portal

Log in to your Client Portal.

From the top navigation, click on the:

Learning tab

If you do not see the Learning tab, you will need to enable it.

Step 2 — Enable the Learning Tab (If Needed)

Go to:

Settings → Permissions

Toggle on the Learning option.

Once enabled, return to your client portal and open the Learning tab.

Step 3 — Add Your Course

Inside the Learning tab:

  1. Click Add Course

  2. Select the course you want to add

Your course will now be available within the portal.

Step 4 — Set Course Access

You can control who has access to your course.

Options include:

All Members
The course is available to everyone inside your client portal.

Purchase Required
The course is unlocked after someone buys it.

Drip Access
The course unlocks after a set number of days.

Private Channel Access
Only members inside a specific group or community can access the course.

Invite Only
The course remains private and can only be accessed by users you invite.

Next Step

Once your course is added, you can:

• Connect it to a product for purchase
• Add it to a community or program
• Automate access through workflows
