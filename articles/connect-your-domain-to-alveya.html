Connect Your Domain to Alvéya™

Updated June 2026
Back to Getting Started

Your domain is your online address — it’s how clients find and trust you. Connecting your custom domain to Alvéya™ means that your booking pages, funnels, and website will appear under your brand (e.g., www.yourclinic.com) instead of a generic link.

This guide will walk you through connecting your domain step by step.

Before You Begin

You’ll need: A domain you’ve already purchased (e.g., from GoDaddy, Namecheap, SiteGround, VentraIP, etc.) Access to your domain provider’s DNS settings Your Alvéya™ login details

Step 1: Add Your Domain in Alvéya™

1. Navigate to Domain Setup

  • There are two ways to access the domain setup area:

    • Option 1: Go to Website and Funnels and click on the settings cogwheel.

    • Option 2: Scroll down to Settings on the left-hand side and click on Domains and URL redirects.
  • Both options lead to the same screen. 

2. Connect Your Domain

  • If you already have a domain name, click on Connect a Domain.

    (Do not select “Transfer In”)

  • Choose where you want to connect the domain (e.g., a funnel) and input your domain name.

3. Input Domain Name

  • Copy and paste your domain name from your domain host (e.g., Ventra IP) to ensure accuracy.
  • Click Continue. 

4. Choose to Add Record Manually

    • You will see your domain name under the URL.
    • Select Add Record Manually for most domain hosts. 

Step 2: Update Your DNS Records In your Domain Host

Access DNS Settings

  • Go back to your domain host and navigate to the DNS settings.
  • You will see standard DNS records that need to be replaced or added. 

Add A Record

  • Start with the A record:

    • Copy the required value and include the ‘@’ sign.

    • Choose A record, input ‘@’, and the IP address.
  • If you encounter an error stating the DNS hostname ‘@’ is not available, add the record without any input, and it should automatically recognize it. 

Add CNAME Record for www

  • Next, add the CNAME record for ‘www’:

    • Copy the required value for the CNAME.

    • Choose CNAME and input the value.

Remove Conflicting Records

  • If you receive an error about existing DNS records, remove the conflicting records before adding the new ones.

Step 3: Verify and finalise set up in Alvéya

Verify DNS Records

  • After adding the records, click Verify to check if everything is set up correctly.
  • Wait for verification to complete.

Connect Domain to Product

  • Once verified, you can connect your domain to the desired product (e.g., sales funnel, website, store, blog, or webinar).
  • Optionally, set a 404 page if you have one created.

Finalise Setup

  • Select the homepage for your website and click Proceed to Finish.

Manage Domain Records

  • Your domain name will now be listed under External Domains.
  • To amend the domain settings, click on Manage Records.

Step 4: Test Your Connection

  • Open your browser and type in your domain.
  • Check that it loads your Alvéya™ page (e.g., booking page, funnel, or website).
  • If you see “Not Secure,” give it 5–10 minutes for SSL to finish applying.

    Troubleshooting Tips

    • Propagation can take time → DNS changes may take up to 24–48 hours, though it’s usually faster.

    • Check that you chose "Connect" not "Transfer In"

    • Double-check spelling → Typos in your domain or DNS records will break the connection.
    • Clear your cache → If you’ve just made updates, refresh with a cleared browser cache or try incognito mode.

    Still Stuck?

    • Check our DNS Troubleshooting Guide.
    • Use the chat widget inside Alvéya™ for live support.
    • Or contact your domain provider for DNS-specific help - unfortunatley we cannot connect the domain name for you.
