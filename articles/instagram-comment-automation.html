Instagram Comment Automation (Similar to ManyChat)

Updated June 2026
Back to Automations

This guide walks you through connecting your Facebook and Instagram accounts to Alvéya and activating comment and direct message automations — so you can respond to your audience instantly, deliver freebies or lead magnets on autopilot, and follow up with interested prospects without lifting a finger.

Before you start:  You will need to be the admin of your Facebook Page and have an Instagram Business or Creator account connected to it. Personal Instagram accounts cannot be used with automation — make sure this is set up in Facebook before you begin.

Step 1: Connect Your Facebook Integration

The first thing you need to do is link your Facebook account to Alvéya. This is what gives the platform permission to send messages, read comments, and trigger automations on your behalf.

Navigate to the Facebook integration settings

  • From the left-hand menu, go to Settings.
  • Scroll down to the Integrations section and select Facebook.
  • Click Connect and log in with the Facebook account that manages your Page.
  • When prompted, select the Facebook Page you want to connect. If you manage multiple Pages, make sure you choose the correct one.

💡  If you do not see your Page listed, make sure you are logged in to the correct Facebook account — the one where you hold admin access to the Page.

Step 2: Grant Full Messaging and Comment Permissions

This is the step most people rush through — and it is where automations break down. Facebook will present you with a list of permissions to grant Alvéya. You need to enable all of them, not just the ones that seem relevant.

Here is what each permission does and why it is required:

Permission

Why it matters

Manage and access Page conversations in Messenger

Allows Alvéya to send and receive DMs on your Facebook Page

Show a list of the Pages you manage

Lets Alvéya identify which Page to connect to

Manage your ads and their data

Required for some campaign-triggered automations

Access profile and posts on Instagram

Allows Alvéya to monitor Instagram posts for comment triggers

Read and reply to Instagram comments

Enables automated replies to post comments

Send messages to Instagram

Allows Alvéya to send DMs to users who trigger a workflow

⚠️  Do not skip or toggle off any of these permissions. If Alvéya does not have full access, your automations will either fail silently or only partially fire. If you are unsure whether you granted everything correctly, disconnect and reconnect your Facebook integration and go through the permissions screen again.

Connecting your Instagram account

During the Facebook connection process, you will also be prompted to connect your Instagram account. Because Instagram is owned by Meta, it connects through your Facebook Page.

  • When prompted, select your Instagram Business or Creator account from the list.
  • Ensure the Instagram account shown is connected to your Facebook Page. If it is not linked yet, you will need to do this in your Facebook Page settings first.
  • Grant all Instagram permissions when prompted — including the ability to read comments and send DMs.

💡  To check or update your Instagram-to-Facebook Page link: open Facebook, go to your Page settings, select Linked Accounts, and connect your Instagram from there. Come back to Alvéya once that is done.

Step 3: Find the Pre-Built Automation Template

Alvéya includes a ready-made workflow template for social media comment and DM automation — you do not need to build this from scratch. Here is how to access it:

  • From the left-hand menu, go to Automation and select Workflows.
  • Click on the Alvéya Workflow Templates Folder and then to the Social Media Workflows Folder
  • Duplicate the Instagram Comment Automation template and rename it so you can keep a clean copy
  • Alternatively, choose “add new” and then Search for “Social Media” or “Comment Automation” to find the pre-built template.
  • Give your new workflow a name that reflects what it is for — for example, “Instagram Freebie DM” or “Facebook Comment Reply”.

💡  The template is pre-configured with the most common trigger and action structure. Your job is simply to customise the trigger keyword, the message content, and the post it applies to — not to rebuild the logic from scratch.

Step 4: Set Up Your Comment Triggers

With the template open, you will now customise it to match your specific campaign.

Configuring a comment trigger

Use this trigger when you want to automatically respond to people who comment on a specific post — for example, sending a resource via DM when someone comments a particular keyword.

  • Click on the trigger step in the workflow.
  • Search for “comment” and choose the platform you want (Instagram or Facebook)
  • If you want both Instagram and facebook, add a second triggar next to this first one and both will act as a trigger.
  • Set the first filter up as “page is” and chose your account
  • Set a second filter as either Contains phrase or Exact match
  • In the Keyword field, enter the word or phrase that should activate the automation — for example, “FREE” or “SEND IT”. Leave this blank if you want any comment to trigger it.
  • Best practice is to toggle on first level comments only

⚠️  Be thoughtful about using “any comment” as your trigger on popular posts. If a post goes viral, every comment will activate the workflow. Using a specific keyword gives you more control and makes the automation feel more intentional to the recipient.

  • NOTE: as long as this workflow is published, anytime anyone comments that word on any post, it will trigger. If you want to allocate a specific post only, then add a “post is published” filter

Writing your automated message

Once your trigger is configured, the next step in the template is the  message action. 

Click on it to customise:

    • Keep the message short, warm, and action-oriented. The person just interacted with you publicly — your DM is continuing that conversation.
    • Include the link or resource you are delivering, if applicable.
    • Use personalisation tokens like {{contact.first_name}} to make the message feel direct.
    • If this is the start of a longer sequence, the template will have additional steps below — update each one with your own content.

Step 5: Test Your Automation

Before you go live, always test your automation so you know it is working correctly. There are two ways to do this:

Test using the Workflow Test button

  • Inside the workflow editor, click the Test Workflow button in the top right corner.
  • Select a test contact (use yourself or a test contact you have set up for this purpose).
  • Confirm the test trigger and watch the workflow run through each step.
  • Check the conversation inbox and the contact record to confirm the message was sent and the contact was updated correctly.

Test live from Instagram or Facebook

  • Make sure your workflow is set to Active.
  • Go to the post you connected and comment the trigger keyword from a personal or test account.
  • Within a minute or two, that account should receive an automated DM.
  • If no DM arrives, check the workflow history in Alvéya to see where it stopped — the error will usually point to a permissions issue or a disconnected integration.

⚠️  If your test DM does not arrive, the most common cause is a missing permission from Step 2. Go back to Settings > Facebook, disconnect the integration, and reconnect it — making sure every permission toggle is switched on this time.

Common Issues and Quick Fixes

Further Help Documents:

Instagram ↔ Alvéya Troubleshooting

Connecting Instagram and Facebook for Automations

Issue

What to check

Automation not triggering at all

Check that the workflow is set to Active and that the Facebook integration is still connected in Settings

DM is being sent but not arriving

Facebook or Instagram may have restricted your account for sending too many DMs too quickly — add a delay step and reduce frequency

Instagram account not appearing in settings

Confirm your Instagram account is linked to your Facebook Page in Meta Business Suite, then reconnect the integration in Alvéya

Keyword trigger not firing

Make sure the keyword in the comment matches exactly what you entered in the trigger — it is not case-sensitive but must be an exact word match

Workflow running multiple times per person

Check the "Times a contact can go through" setting in the trigger — set it to Once Per Post or Once to prevent repeats
