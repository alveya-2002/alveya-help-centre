Automate Appointment Reminders in Alvéya™

Updated June 2026
Back to Automations

Automated appointment reminders help reduce no-shows and make client communication seamless.
In Alvéya™, reminders are managed through Automations, where you can set emails or SMS messages to go out automatically before (or after) a booking.

Step 1: Open or Create a Workflow

  1. Go to Automations → Workflows.

  2. Click + Create Workflow (or open an existing one if you already have an appointment reminder flow).

  3. Choose Start from Scratch and name it something like Appointment Reminder – Naturopathic Consults.

Step 2: Set the Trigger

  1. Click + Add New Trigger.

  2. From the dropdown list, choose Appointment Status.

3. Under Workflow Trigger Name, enter something like Appointment Booked.

4. Add a Filter → Status Is = Confirmed (or Booked depending on your naming).

5. Click Save Trigger.

💡 This tells Alvéya™ to start the workflow whenever an appointment is booked or confirmed.

Step 3: Add the Email to the workflow

  1. Click the + that is below the trigger step, connected with the line.
  2. Type in : Send email to search for the Send email step.
  3. Write your email confirmation or if you would like to create a template. Save your progress by click Save in the top right corner and exit out of the automation to create an email template

Step 3: Add a Wait Step (Timing the Reminder)

  1. Click + Add Action under your trigger.

  2. Select Wait.

  3. Choose how long before the appointment you’d like to send the reminder, such as:

    • 1 day before

    • 3 hours before

    • 15 minutes before

  4. Click Save Action.

💡 You can add multiple wait steps if you want several reminders — for example, one email the day before and one SMS an hour before.

Step 4: Test the Booking Flow

  1. Visit your live website.
  2. Click the button or use the embedded calendar/service menu.
  3. Make a test booking to confirm:
    • The booking form works correctly.
    • The booking appears in your calendar.
    • Notifications are sent to both you and the client.

Troubleshooting

  • Booking form not showing? Double-check you pasted the correct embed code.
  • Layout looks off? Adjust the width/height settings in your website editor.
  • Mobile display issues? Consider using the Booking Link instead of embed for a smoother mobile experience.
