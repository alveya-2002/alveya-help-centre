Start to finish tutorial to create an opt in funnel

Updated June 2026
Back to Courses & Funnels

This article is step one from the Funnel Playbook. To continue the process of setting up your funnel after this step, head back to the playbook here.

Follow these four steps to build a complete opt-in funnel inside Alvéya — from your sign-up form through to the automated email sequence that delivers your freebie and nurtures new subscribers.

Step 1 — Create Your Opt-In Form

Your form is the starting point of the entire funnel. Alvéya includes ready-made template forms, so you do not need to build one from scratch.

1.1  Duplicate the template form

  • Navigate to Websites and Funnels.
  • Locate the pre-built template forms that have already been created for you.
  • Click the three dots next to the template form and select Duplicate.
  • Move the duplicated form out of the Templates folder: click the three dots again, choose Move to Folder, then select Root Folder.

💡  Always duplicate rather than editing the original template. This keeps a clean master version in place for future opt-ins, and ensures you receive any platform updates without losing your work.

1.2  Customise the form

  • Click on your duplicated form to open the editor.
  • To remove the default shadow or border, go to Styles and Options > Advanced, then turn off the border and set the shadow to zero.
  • Edit field labels to suit your opt-in (e.g. rename or remove the Last Name field if you do not need it).
  • Add any additional form fields you require.
  • Link your Privacy Policy. Remove the Terms of Service checkbox if it is not relevant.
  • Click Save when finished.

1.3  Set up form notifications (optional)

You can receive an email notification each time someone submits the form. Navigate to the Notifications tab within the form settings to configure this. If you are expecting a high volume of sign-ups, it is best to leave notifications off to avoid cluttering your inbox.

1.4  Embedding the form on an external website (if applicable)

If your website is hosted outside of Alvéya, you can embed the form using a code snippet:

  • Open the form and click Integrate.
  • Choose Inline.
  • Click Get Embed Code.
  • Copy the code and paste it into an HTML block on your external website.

Step 2 - Build and customise your funnel page (opt-in + confirmation)

Your funnel has two pages: the opt-in page (where people sign up) and the confirmation page (where they land immediately after submitting the form). Both are pre-built as templates inside Alvéya.

2.1  Duplicate the funnel template

  • From Websites and Funnels, find the opt-in funnel template.
  • Click the three dots and select Clone (this is the duplicate function for funnels).
  • Rename the funnel to match your opt-in offer.
  • Move it out of the Templates folder into the Root Folder to keep things organised.

2.2  Customise the opt-in page

  • Open your funnel and click on the opt-in page to edit it.
  • Scroll to the section containing the form and select the form you created in Step 1.
  • Update all copywriting. The template includes swipe copy in brackets — replace these sections with your own words, or rewrite the page entirely if you prefer.
  • Replace any placeholder images. Images highlighted in orange are standalone image blocks; images highlighted in green are section backgrounds. Both can be swapped out.
  • Check all call-to-action buttons. They should be set to scroll up to the form. Confirm this is working correctly.
  • When you are happy with the layout, review it in different screen sizes using the layout mode.

💡  You do not need to choose a template that already matches your branding. Focus on the layout and structure. A simple change of colours, fonts, and images is all it takes to make any template feel like yours.

2.3  A note on navigation menus in funnels

If your Alvéya website uses a saved global menu, note that funnels are treated as a separate website. Any links in your menu must use the full URL (e.g. www.yourwebsite.com/about) rather than a relative link (e.g. /about). Refer to the dedicated navigation tutorial for more detail on this.

2.4  Customise the confirmation page

The confirmation page appears immediately after someone submits the form. Use it to:

  • Confirm that sign-up was successful so the subscriber is not left wondering what happened.
  • Prompt them to check their junk or spam folder for the delivery email.
  • Let them know you will be in touch over the coming days with helpful content.
  • Include a special offer or upsell if relevant to your opt-in.

Step 3 - Configure your funnel page settings

Before publishing, each page in your funnel needs to be configured correctly. There are three areas where settings are applied: the main funnel settings panel, the individual funnel step settings, and the SEO settings within the page editor.

3.1  Main funnel settings

  • Open the funnel and click Settings on the far right-hand side.
  • Update the funnel name and URL path — this is the link you will share or add to your website.
  • Connect your funnel domain name.
  • Upload a favicon via your media storage, copy the file link, and paste it into the Favicon URL field.
  • Add a Google Analytics tracking code in the Header Tracking Code field if required.
  • Enable a chat widget if you would like one to appear on the page.
  • Use the Events tab to add a Facebook or Meta Pixel event if you are running paid ads.
  • Click Save.

3.2  Funnel step settings

  • Click on the individual funnel step (opt-in page or confirmation page) and open the Publishing tab.
  • Update the step name and URL slug if needed.
  • If you are selling something through the funnel, add it as a product here before placing an order form element on the page.
  • Check the form redirect: click on the form element on the page and confirm the Redirection is set to Go to Next Step. This ensures the visitor is taken to the confirmation page after submitting.

⚠️  Always check that the form redirect is set to Go to Next Step — not Use Action from Form Builder. This is easy to miss and will prevent the confirmation page from loading correctly after sign-up.

3.3  SEO settings

  • Open the page editor and click SEO and AI Search Optimisation.
  • Add a page title (50–60 characters) and meta description (150–160 characters) using keywords your ideal client would search for.
  • Add your author name and any relevant keywords.
  • Upload a social sharing image — design a square image in Canva with your key content centred, so it displays correctly across different social platforms.
  • Repeat the SEO settings on the confirmation page as well.

💡  For a full walkthrough of every setting, refer to the complete guide: help.alveya.io/configuring-funnel-settings

Step 4 - Set up your automation workflow to deliver the opt-in

Your automation workflow is what delivers your freebie and nurtures new subscribers automatically. Alvéya includes a pre-built nurture email workflow template — you do not need to create one from scratch.

4.1  Duplicate the workflow template

  • Navigate to Automation Workflows.
  • Locate the Nurture Email Workflow template.
  • Duplicate it and rename it to match your opt-in offer.
  • Click on the workflow title to open it.

4.2  Set the trigger

The trigger tells Alvéya when to start this workflow. Set it to activate when your specific opt-in form is submitted:

  • Click on the trigger block within the workflow.
  • Select Form Submitted as the trigger type.
  • Filter by the specific form you created in Step 1 (e.g. Example Opt-In Form).
  • Optionally, add a topic-specific tag (e.g. gut-health or fertility) so you can send targeted campaigns to this audience later.

4.3  Customise the delivery email (Email 1)

The first email delivers your freebie. Click the three dots on this email and select Edit Design to customise it:

  • Update the colours, images, and copywriting to match your brand.
  • If your opt-in is a PDF, host it on Google Drive or similar cloud storage and link to it using a button. Do not attach the file directly to the email — attachments can trigger spam filters and slow down delivery. Set the file to view-only so subscribers can download it from your cloud storage.
  • Thank the subscriber, share the key benefits of the download, and include a brief introduction to who you are.
  • Customise or save your email footer as a global footer so you do not need to recreate it for every email.
  • Update the email subject line and preview text by clicking the three dots > Settings.
  • Send a test email to yourself before activating the workflow.

💡  Hosting your PDF on Google Drive rather than attaching it directly can significantly improve email deliverability. It also loads faster for your subscriber. Always set the sharing permissions to View Only.

4.4  Customise the nurture email sequence

The workflow includes a series of follow-up emails spaced out over several days. This sequence helps new subscribers get to know you before your next newsletter arrives in their inbox.

  • Email 2 is formatted to look like a personal email sent directly from you — it is designed to feel conversational rather than promotional.
  • Email 3 offers a quick win or practical next step relevant to your opt-in topic.
  • Email 4 shares a testimonial or a client story that illustrates the transformation you help people achieve.
  • The wait times between emails are set to one day by default. You can adjust these to two days if you prefer. Aim to stay in contact every one to two days during the first week so subscribers begin to recognise your name.

4.5  Add the newsletter tag at the end of the workflow

The final step in the workflow adds a Newsletter tag to each subscriber. This moves them from the opt-in nurture sequence into your general audience. When you send a regular email campaign or newsletter, direct it to this Newsletter tag only — this prevents subscribers who are still mid-way through the nurture sequence from receiving two emails in quick succession.

Step 5 - Test everything from start to finish

Before sharing your opt-in with your audience, run through the entire journey yourself to confirm everything works as expected.

  • Publish your funnel page (or navigate to the page on your external website where the form is embedded).
  • Fill in the form using your own details.
  • Confirm that you land on the correct confirmation page.
  • Check that you receive the first delivery email in your inbox.
  • Verify that the download link in the email works and opens your freebie correctly.
  • To test the full email sequence in one sitting, temporarily change the wait times from one day to one minute. Let all emails send through, check each one looks correct, then change the wait times back to your original settings before going live.
