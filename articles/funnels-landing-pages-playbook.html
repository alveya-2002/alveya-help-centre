Funnels & Landing Pages Playbook

Updated June 2026
Back to Getting Started

A Complete Setup Guide for Alvéya™

Funnels and landing pages are designed to guide visitors toward a specific action. Whether that action is booking an appointment, downloading a free resource, or purchasing a product, a funnel removes distractions and creates a clear path.

Unlike a website, which allows open navigation, a funnel is intentional and structured. When built correctly, it improves conversions, tracks performance accurately, and connects directly to your automations and payments.

This Playbook walks you through creating and launching a funnel from start to finish.

What You’ll Complete in This Guide

You’ll move through the following steps:

  1. Create Your Funnel
  2. Build & Structure Your Pages
  3. Configure Forms or Checkout
  4. Connect Automations
  5. Set Domain & URL Paths
  6. Test & Publish

Work through these steps in order to ensure your funnel functions correctly.

Before You Begin

Before building your funnel, ensure:

  • Your email sending is configured (if collecting leads)
  • Your calendar is set up (if taking bookings)
  • Your payment processor is connected (if selling)
  • Your domain is ready (if publishing on a custom URL)

Steps to setting up Funnels & Landing Pages

Step 1 – Create Your Funnel

Overview

Go to Sites → Funnels.
Click Create Funnel.

You can:

  • Choose a blank funnel
  • Use a template
  • Import a shared funnel

Name your funnel clearly so it’s easy to identify later.

A funnel is made up of individual steps. Each step represents one page in the journey, such as:

  • Opt-in page
  • Sales page
  • Checkout page
  • Thank you page

📖 Understanding Funnel Structure →

Step 2 – Build & Structure Your Pages

Overview

Open each funnel step and customise the layout using sections, rows, columns, and elements.

Update:

  • Headlines
  • Copy
  • Images
  • Buttons
  • Spacing

Always check both desktop and mobile views before saving.

If needed, you can:

  • Duplicate steps
  • Reorder steps
  • Rename steps

📖 Page Builder Overview →
📖 Sections, Rows & Elements Explained →

Step 3 – Configure Forms or Checkout

Overview

This step connects your funnel to a real action.

If collecting leads:

  • Add a Form element
  • Connect it to the correct form
  • Ensure submission triggers are configured

If selling a product:

  • Add a Checkout element
  • Select your product
  • Confirm pricing and payment processor

If offering bookings:

  • Add a Calendar element
  • Select the correct calendar

Test the action immediately after configuring it.

Step 4 – Connect Automations

Overview

Funnels work best when connected to workflows.

Common automations include:

  • Sending confirmation emails
  • Adding a contact tag
  • Triggering a nurture sequence
  • Sending SMS confirmations

Go to Automations → Workflows and create or connect a trigger such as:

  • Form Submitted
  • Order Submitted
  • Appointment Booked

Test the workflow before moving forward.

📖 Automations & Workflows Playbook →

Step 5 – Set Domain & URL Paths

Overview

Each funnel step requires a clean URL.

You can:

  • Publish under your main domain
  • Use a subdomain
  • Use the default system URL for testing

Go to Settings → Domains to connect your domain if needed.

If publishing under your main website domain, ensure paths are unique.

📖 Connecting Your Domain →

📖 DNS Setup by Provider →

Step 6 – Test & Publish

Overview

Before going live:

  • Click through each step in order
  • Submit a test form
  • Complete a test purchase (if selling)
  • Book a test appointment (if applicable)
  • Confirm emails and SMS are received
  • Check mobile layout

When ready, publish the funnel.

If changes are not appearing, confirm the step has been published.
